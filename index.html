The Hidden Geography of Wildfire Displacement

Multi-source event-level panel (1,648 events, 87 countries, 2019–2024) combining NASA FIRMS satellite data with IDMC displacement records to test whether fire intensity predicts human displacement. Fire size explains only ~10% of variance; the relationship inverts in low-income countries — a reporting-capacity signal, not a damage signal.

Team of 2 · Graduate Research · Under preparation for submission

Multi-Layered Seismic Vulnerability of Urban Road Networks

A network-science framework for four high-risk districts of western Istanbul that integrates physical road fragility, expected earthquake loss, and socio-demographic vulnerability under a Mw 7.5 scenario. Uses OSMnx-derived road graphs (1,449 nodes, 2,248 edges) and 1,000-iteration Monte Carlo simulation to show that topologically critical edges and structurally vulnerable edges are essentially independent sets.
