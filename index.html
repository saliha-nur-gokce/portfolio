Climate Shocks & Agricultural Prices

Hybrid pipeline integrating Gradient Boosting threshold detection with panel fixed-effects regression to quantify how frost shocks propagate into Turkish producer prices across 81 provinces (2014–2024). Key finding: a −2°C shock raises perishable vegetable prices by 6.5% with a one-month lag.

The Hidden Geography of Wildfire Displacement

Multi-source event-level panel (1,648 events, 87 countries, 2019–2024) combining NASA FIRMS satellite data with IDMC displacement records to test whether fire intensity predicts human displacement. Fire size explains only ~10% of variance; the relationship inverts in low-income countries — a reporting-capacity signal, not a damage signal.
